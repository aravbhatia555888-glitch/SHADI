## 1. 💍 The Knot (Market Leader - $200M+ revenue)

### What They Show on Vendor Profiles:

#### ❌ What They DON'T Show:
- No direct phone number
- No direct email address
- No personal contact info

#### ✅ What They DO Show:
```
Vendor Profile:
├── Photos & Videos
├── Reviews & Ratings
├── Price Range ($$$ - not exact)
├── Services Offered
├── Location/Service Area
└── [Contact Vendor] Button ← THIS IS KEY
```

### How Contact Works:
```
User clicks "Contact Vendor"
  ↓
Popup form appears:
  - Wedding Date
  - Guest Count
  - Budget Range
  - Message
  - User Email/Phone
  ↓
The Knot sends inquiry to vendor
  ↓
Vendor pays The Knot $30-50 for this lead
  ↓
Vendor responds through The Knot platform
  ↓
After initial contact, vendor CAN share direct contact
```

### Vendor Pricing (Real Numbers):
- **Basic Listing**: FREE (but buried in search)
- **Starter**: $50/month (better placement)
- **Standard**: $150/month (featured, more leads)
- **Premium**: $300/month (top placement, unlimited leads)
- **Per Lead**: $30-50 each (if not on subscription)

### How They Make Money:
1. **Vendor Subscriptions**: 80% of revenue
2. **Lead Fees**: 15% of revenue
3. **Advertising**: 5% of revenue

## 🎯 Key Insights: What They ALL Do

### 1. **No Direct Contact Info Shown**
- ❌ No phone numbers visible
- ❌ No email addresses visible
- ❌ No website links (or limited)

### 2. **Forced Through Platform**
- ✅ All initial contact through their system
- ✅ They track every inquiry
- ✅ They charge for every lead

### 3. **Value Exchange**
**For Users:**
- "Get quotes from multiple vendors"
- "Compare prices easily"
- "Read verified reviews"
- "Secure booking"

**For Vendors:**
- "Get qualified leads"
- "Serious couples only"
- "Manage all inquiries in one place"
- "Get paid faster"

### 4. **After First Contact**
Once vendor responds, they CAN share direct contact, but:
- Conversation often stays in-app
- Booking encouraged through platform
- Platform takes commission on bookings

## 🔒 How They Prevent Bypass

### Smart Tactics:

1. **Value-Added Services**
   - Reviews (vendors want good reviews)
   - Analytics (vendors want to see performance)
   - Booking management (easier than email)
   - Payment processing (secure for both sides)

2. **Vendor Lock-In**
   - "You're already paying $200/month"
   - "All your leads are here"
   - "Your reviews are here"
   - "Your calendar is here"
   - Hard to leave!

3. **User Benefits**
   - Compare multiple vendors easily
   - See all conversations in one place
   - Secure payment
   - Dispute resolution
   - Review system

4. **Contract Terms**
   - Vendors agree not to bypass platform
   - Risk losing their listing
   - Risk losing reviews
   - Legal consequences

## 💡 Pro Tips from The Leaders

### 1. **Start Free, Then Charge**
- Let vendors list for free initially
- Build user base first
- Then introduce paid tiers
- "Upgrade for more visibility"

### 2. **Freemium Works**
- Free: Basic listing, pay per lead
- Paid: Better placement, included leads

### 3. **Data is Gold**
- Track everything
- Show vendors analytics
- "You got 50 views this week!"
- "Your response rate is 80%"
- Makes them feel invested

### 4. **Reviews are Sticky**
- Vendors won't leave if they have 100+ reviews
- Reviews = social proof = more business
- Vendors protect their reviews

### 5. **Make It Easy**
- Don't make it hard to use
- Simple inquiry forms
- Quick responses
- Mobile-friendly
